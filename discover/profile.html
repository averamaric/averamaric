Beyond the CV

Hi, I'm Analucia.

I was born in La Paz, Bolivia, and hold Bolivian, Chilean, and Ecuadorian nationality, three passports that could, in theory, make me a very convincing spy.

My path into automation started early: as a kid I kept a notebook full of inventions, and it was the first sign I wanted to be an engineer. Later, working with IoT I fell in love with PLCs, automation lines, and everything Industry 4.0. The way automation and other tools can genuinely transform a process is still what excites me most about this field.
